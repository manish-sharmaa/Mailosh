C · Graphite & Blue

Neutral graphite with a classic mail-app blue. The most familiar to Gmail and Outlook refugees; the least distinctive — it borrows their trust and a little of their sameness.

Whichever you choose becomes the shipped default theme; the token system makes additional accents cheap later (Superhuman has twelve). Light follows the OS unless overridden in Quick settings.
