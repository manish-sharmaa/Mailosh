Type is Inter at 13 px; rows are 44 px (the "Default" density); radii 6–10 px; one accent. Nothing here is final colour — that's the next screen.

One decision: the default reading-pane mode on wide screens

Users can switch this in Quick settings; below ~1100 px everyone gets the list-first single pane. Click to choose.

A

Right of the list (Recommended)

What you see above. The convention of Fastmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and ihasmail; Gmail offers it. Triage and read without losing your place.

B

No split — conversation replaces the list

Gmail's default and Superhuman's "one thing at a time". Wider rows and a calmer screen; costs a keystroke (u) to get back.

C

Below the list

Outlook-classic. Full-width rows for long subjects; shallower reading area.
