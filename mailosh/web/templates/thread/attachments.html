{# Attachment chips (spec §7), one per downloadable part of `message`. Context: `message`, a `mailosh.services.conversation.MessageView`. It self-guards on `message.attachments`, so a message with nothing attached renders nothing at all — not an empty row with a border on it. `MessageView.attachments` has already dropped the parts that are not attachments: a part with a `cid` is the sender's inline logo, referenced by their own HTML, and a part with no `blobId` cannot be fetched. Neither decision is re-made here; this file draws what that list contains. --------------------------------------------------------------------- Two real controls, not one hover affordance Download and Preview are `` elements that are in the tab order at all times. `.chip-actions` fades them in on `:hover` AND on `:focus-within` (styles/input.css), which is the whole reason the reveal is `opacity` rather than `display: none` — a control removed from the box tree cannot be focused, so it could never bring itself into view, and spec §11 is explicit that a hover action has to be keyboard-operable. 1A fixed exactly this bug on list rows. Each carries one accessible name, in one place: a `.sr-only` span, with the glyph left `aria-hidden` by `icon()`. Deliberately not *also* a `title` — two copies of "Download spec.pdf" is two things to keep in step, and a screen reader that reads both announces the file twice. --------------------------------------------------------------------- Preview is offered only for what the server will actually show `AttachmentView.preview` is non-`None` exactly for the types `GET /m/{id}/att/{blob}?inline=1` serves with their real `Content-Type` (`conversation.PREVIEW_KIND`, held identical to `frames.PREVIEW_TYPES` by a test). Everything else — `.zip`, and `text/html` and `image/svg+xml` above all — that route quietly downgrades to a download, so a "Preview" on one of those would be a control whose label is a lie about what it does. A chip for one of them carries Download and nothing else: spec §7's "unsupported types fall back to download" is this absence, not a dialog that opens and says it cannot show the file. --------------------------------------------------------------------- The whole chip is the preview control `.chip-link` is stretched over the chip by styles/thread.css, exactly as `.row-link` is stretched over a list row and for the same reason: what the reader means by clicking a file is "show me this file", and the surface they aim at is the whole chip rather than a 26px glyph revealed on hover. Download stays a control of its own, painted above the stretched link so a click on it is a download and not a preview. **It is a real link to a URL that really works, and only then a dialog.** `static/js/attachments.js` intercepts the click and opens `thread/preview.html`'s `` instead; with that module absent, or on a ⌘/Ctrl/Shift-click, the anchor is left alone and the attachment opens in a tab as it always did.
