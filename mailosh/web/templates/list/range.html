{# The toolbar's range readout ("1–24 of 24") — one span, rendered in two places, which is the whole point of it being its own file. It sits in `list/toolbar.html`, *outside* `#list`, but it describes what is inside `#list`. Every live update replaces only `#list`'s contents (`hx-trigger="mail:changed from:body"`), so the readout went stale on its own: 24 over 23 rows after an archive, 24 over 25 after new mail landed. `list/rows.html` therefore renders this again with `oob=true`, and htmx swaps it by id alongside the rows — the same out-of-band idiom, and the same reason, as `shell/nav.html`'s badges. `range_label` (mailosh.web.mail._range_label) is the only thing that formats it: the en dash, the thousands separators and the "0 of 0" empty case are all server-side, so nothing here or in the client re-derives a number the server already knows. #} {{ range_label }}
