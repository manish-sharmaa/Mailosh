One file, because a grammar written down twice is a grammar that gets half-updated. Every row here is a real operator of the grammar `mailosh.services.search_query` implements — nothing aspirational, and nothing spelled differently from what the parser accepts. `filename:` is deliberately absent: it is *approximated* through free text rather than implemented, and the parser says so with a hint at the moment a reader uses it, which is the honest place to say it. Advertising it here would read as a promise. Inert markup, on purpose. These rows take no focus and carry no handler: they are a reference card sitting inside a popover that is held open by `:focus-within`, so a row that could be clicked would be a row that closes the panel by taking the focus holding it open. #}
